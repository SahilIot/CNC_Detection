# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and
focused on improving the software. Everyone who participates as a contributor,
reviewer, or community member is expected to help maintain a welcoming
environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Assume good intent, ask questions when context is unclear, and focus
  criticism on ideas or code rather than people.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish camera footage, credentials, personal
  information, or other sensitive site data.
- Keep project discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insulting or deliberately disruptive comments or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing another person's private information or project secrets without
  permission.
- Knowingly sharing malicious code, harmful content, or unsafe instructions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code.
They may edit or remove contributions that violate it and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through
GitHub. Do not use a public issue to share private information. Reports will
be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and
project-related communication.
